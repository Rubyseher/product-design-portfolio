# Case study intake — answer inline, anything you skip I'll flag as a gap

Rules of thumb while answering:
- Real numbers beat adjectives. "7 of 9 people" > "most people".
- One verbatim quote per finding is worth more than three paraphrases.
- If something didn't happen (no usability test, no personas), write NONE. I'll
  either restructure around it or write it honestly as a limitation. Do not
  invent data — a case study with three real findings reads stronger than one
  with twelve fake ones.
