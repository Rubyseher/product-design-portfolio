Problem

Job seekers track applications across a dozen browser tabs, inboxes, and half finished spreadsheets, with no single place that shows where anything actually stands. [Add context: how many applications a typical active job seeker is juggling, and what prompted you to look into this.]

The problem was never a lack of tools. It was too many tools, and no single source of truth.

Solution

A lightweight tracker built for the job search: every application in one view, status updates that take seconds, and enough structure to actually trust it. [One or two sentences on the core mechanic of your solution.]

Design goals

I translated the research into a short list of principles that shaped every screen after this point.

One source of truth

  • Every application lives in exactly one place
  • No duplicate tracking across tools

Fast status updates

  • Update a status in under two seconds
  • No form to fill out for a one line change

Zero setup friction

  • Useful from the first application added
  • No template to configure before it works
